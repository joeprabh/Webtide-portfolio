## ⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTES

### Zone Overlap vs Confluence

**Overlap** = Zones are close but not exact
- Trade the better quality zone
- Use the other for confirmation

**Confluence** = Zones at EXACT same level
- Highest probability
- Institutional validation from 2 methods

### Touched = Deleted

```
Price touches ANY zone → Auto-deleted
├─ S&D zones: Delete on touch
├─ OB zones: Delete on touch
└─ No second chances (keep "Show Touched" OFF)
```

If you have difficulty with colors:
- Change S&D to Blue/Purple
